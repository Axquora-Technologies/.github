## How we work

1. **Understand the goal** — align technology with the business outcome.
2. **Design for reality** — choose the simplest architecture that can scale.
3. **Build with care** — keep quality, security and maintainability visible throughout delivery.
4. **Improve continuously** — measure, learn and keep systems healthy after launch.

## What matters to us

- **Reliability over hype:** technology should solve a real problem and keep solving it.
- **Security by design:** protection is part of the engineering process, not an afterthought.
- **Clarity and ownership:** communicate directly, document decisions and follow through.
- **Practical innovation:** use the right tool for the job, not the newest tool by default.
